Your first résumé

Six steps, one conversation.

Download your LinkedIn data

On LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy, then Data privacy, then Get a copy of your data. LinkedIn emails you a .zip within about 10 minutes. An old résumé (PDF, Word, or text) works too.

You don't need to unzip it.

Approve every line

For each line: Approve, Edit, Reject, or Later. Your AI never invents a title, a number, or a client. If you didn't say it, it doesn't go on your résumé.

Keep it current

Say "add this to my résumé" when you finish something.

It goes into your résumé inbox, and nothing on your résumé changes yet. Your AI scores it honestly. Work that clears the bar becomes a suggested line, often replacing a weaker one so your résumé stays at two pages. Anything else gets a plain "not yet, and here's why."

Say add this to my résumé

Say catch up my résumé and your AI suggests items from what it already knows

Say set up my design engineer résumé for a version per kind of job

Each version only chooses and orders your approved lines. It never writes anything new, so every version stays true.

Job matches · Pro

A short list of jobs that fit, every week.

Say set up my job search. Your AI asks nine quick questions: titles, contract or full-time, remote, pay, dealbreakers, which companies to watch, and how often. Then you get a page of the jobs that fit your approved résumé best, with an Apply button on each.

The search runs on your computer and reads only the public job listings of companies you choose. It never reads your email and never applies for you.

Say prepare on a match for a version tailored to that job

Say help me reach someone there for the posting, the likely person, and a short note you send

Running it on your computer's own timer uses no AI at all. Your AI's optional one-line review of each match uses a small amount.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. In the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app you only talk. Your AI runs everything and hands you the files.

What do I do next?

Every conversation ends with one clear next step, so you always know what to do.

Where is my data?

In the folder you chose, on your computer. Nothing is published until you publish it.

Will it make things up?

No. Every public line traces to something you said or a file you gave it, and you approve each one.

What does it cost?

Your résumé, every format, and one tailored version are free. Pro adds job matches, unlimited versions, and job-by-job tailoring.
