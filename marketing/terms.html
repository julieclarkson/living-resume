What Living Résumé is

Living Résumé is software from Jacobus Company, LLC that helps you keep a résumé you approve line by line, build it in several formats, and find job listings that fit it. It runs on your computer, inside your AI tool.

Your content

Your résumé and everything in your folder belong to you. You decide what goes on your résumé and what you publish. Living Résumé suggests; you approve.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep what you publish true. Living Résumé is built to never invent claims, but you approve every line and are responsible for it.
  • Follow the rules of the sites you use, such as LinkedIn and job boards. Living Résumé never applies for you or edits your profiles; you do those steps yourself.
  • Don't use Living Résumé to misrepresent yourself or anyone else.

Free and Pro

The free version is yours to use. Pro is a paid license sold through Polar. Prices, renewals, and refunds are shown at checkout and handled under Polar's terms.

No guarantees

Living Résumé is provided as is. It can't promise interviews, jobs, or that any company will read your résumé a certain way. Job listings come from public sources and may be out of date. Always check a listing on the employer's own site before you apply.
