Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Your résumé stays with you

Living Résumé runs on your own computer, inside the AI tool you already use. Your résumé, the notes behind each line, your imports, job matches, and applications are saved as files in a folder you choose. We don't have a copy, and there's no Living Résumé account to sign in to.

What leaves your computer

  • License check (Pro only). When you activate or check a Pro license, your license key and a label for your computer go to Polar, our payment and license provider. Nothing about your résumé is sent.
  • Job matches (Pro only). When you search for jobs, Living Résumé reads the public job listings of the companies you chose, from their public job boards (such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, or the company's own Workday careers site). Only the company's board name and your job titles are sent. Matching happens on your computer.
  • Publishing. Your web page, PDF, and résumé file go online only when you publish them yourself, to the place you choose.

What we never do

  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
  • We never read your email, log in to job sites, or apply or send messages for you.
  • LinkedIn imports read only your profile files (profile, positions, education, skills, certifications, volunteering, email address). Messages, connections, phone numbers, addresses, and birth dates are never opened or stored.

Your AI tool

Living Résumé works inside an AI tool such as Claude or ChatGPT. What you say in those conversations is handled under that tool's own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

Delete your résumé folder, and everything Living Résumé saved is gone. To remove a published page, delete it from the place you published it.
