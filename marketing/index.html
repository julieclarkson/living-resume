How it works

Five minutes a month, inside the tool you already use.

  1. Import what you have

    Your LinkedIn data export or your current résumé. It's scanned before anything reads it.

  2. Review every line

    Approve, edit, reject, or save for later. Nothing is public until you say so.

  3. Build every format

    A two-page PDF that screening software reads cleanly, a Word file, a web page, JSON Resume, and a paste-ready LinkedIn packet.

  4. Publish yourself

    Files land in your site folder. Going live is your own commit, so nothing ships behind your back.

Why it stays current

Your build history is the evidence.

If you capture decisions with Case Study Maker, the monthly scout reads only the moments tagged as launches, milestones, and new techniques. It proposes wording, where the line belongs, and why it helps. It rephrases what's verified and never adds a claim to fit a job post.

Saved job postings tell it which words employers use right now, so your real work is described in the language that gets read.

Privacy and control

Nothing leaves your computer until you publish it.

  • Private evidence notes are never exported.
  • A private-names list blocks internal project, client, and repo names from every public file.
  • No phone number on public surfaces by default, and stray emails are caught.
  • Imported files and job postings are treated as data, never as instructions to your AI.
  • No account, no server, no tracking. The only network call is the Pro license check.

Questions

Straight answers.

How do I keep my résumé updated automatically?

Living Résumé reads the work you already record and proposes updates once a month. You approve them in a few minutes and every format rebuilds from the same source.

Will it make things up?

No. Every public line needs evidence, and new lines are proposals until you approve them.

Can it update LinkedIn for me?

LinkedIn doesn't allow automated profile edits, so you get a paste-ready packet trimmed to LinkedIn's limits, with what changed since last time.

Do recruiters' AI agents see it?

Your web page carries JSON Resume and schema.org Person data at a stable address, which is what search engines and agents read.
