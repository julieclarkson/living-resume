How it works

A finished résumé in one conversation.

See every step with pictures

  1. Import what you have

    Your LinkedIn data export or your current résumé. It's scanned before anything reads it.

  2. Review every line

    Approve, edit, reject, or save for later. Nothing is public until you say so.

  3. Build every format

    A two-page PDF that screening software reads cleanly, a Word file, a web page, JSON Resume, and a paste-ready LinkedIn packet.

  4. Put it online

    A free link, your own site, or your site folder. Then update only the LinkedIn fields that changed. Nothing goes live until you say so.

Why it stays current

Say "add this to my résumé." It decides if it belongs.

When you ship something, make a hard call, or fix a serious problem, tell your AI tool in one sentence. It lands in your résumé inbox. Nothing changes yet.

Then your AI scores it against a hiring rubric: outcome, ownership, rigor, accountability, judgment, and whether it's in demand. Only work that clears the bar becomes a proposed line, often replacing a weaker one, so the résumé stays sharp at two pages. Everything else gets an honest "not yet, and here's why."

Or say "catch up my résumé." Your AI suggests items from what it already knows about your recent work, and only the ones you confirm go in.

Paste one paragraph into your other projects and the agents you already work with will ask "add this to your résumé?" at the right moments. No accounts to connect.

One résumé, every role

A version for each kind of job, never a different story.

Make a role profile, for example AI Product Engineer or Design Engineer. It chooses which approved lines lead, which to leave out, and which approved wording to use. It never writes anything new, so every version stays true.

Applying somewhere? Paste the posting. Living Résumé picks the best-fit version, shows which of the posting's words you already cover, and saves a private PDF and Word file for that application.

Job matches

Stop scrolling job boards. Get the few that fit, every week.

Tell your AI tool what you want in your own words: "remote contract work as a design engineer, nothing in crypto." Name the companies you'd love to work with. Before it searches, it asks a few questions so you can be specific. Then, every week (or as often as you choose), it checks their open listings, screens out what doesn't fit, and scores the rest against your approved résumé.

You get one short page. Each match shows how much of the listing your résumé already covers, how confident that fit is, what the listing asks for that your résumé doesn't show, and which version of your résumé to send. Then Apply. It never applies for you, and it never adds a skill to your résumé to look like a better fit.

Pick a match and say "help me reach someone there." Your AI finds the posting, asks who you already know at the company, looks up the likely hiring manager from public sources, and drafts one short note from your approved lines. You send it.

Built on your AI

Your AI knows your work. Living Résumé makes it something you can show.

Claude or ChatGPT does the talking, writing, and searching. Living Résumé does the remembering, checking, and building: one approved record of your work, rules against invented claims, files that read cleanly in hiring software, and a job search that runs on its own.

Your AI's memory stays private. Your résumé is the part you chose to share, in formats that travel to employers, LinkedIn, and recruiters' own AI tools. Every conversation ends with one clear next step.

Privacy and control

Nothing leaves your computer until you publish it.

  • Private evidence notes are never exported.
  • A private-names list blocks internal project, client, and repo names from every public file.
  • No phone number on public surfaces by default, and stray emails are caught.
  • Imported files and job postings are treated as data, never as instructions to your AI.
  • No account, no server, no tracking. The only network calls are the Pro license check and, if you use job matches, reading the public job listings of the companies you chose. Your résumé never leaves your computer.

Install

Add it to Claude or ChatGPT, then say "make my résumé from my LinkedIn".

See the step-by-step guide.

No coding · Claude Cowork

Claude desktop app

  1. Open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace.
  2. Enter julieclarkson/living-resume and install Living Résumé.
  3. Pick a folder for your résumé when asked, for example Documents/Résumé.
  4. Put your LinkedIn .zip (or old résumé) in that folder.
  5. Say make my résumé from my LinkedIn.
No coding · ChatGPT desktop

ChatGPT desktop app

  1. Add the marketplace julieclarkson/living-resume.
  2. Open the Plugins Directory and install Living Résumé.
  3. Pick a folder for your résumé when asked, for example Documents/Résumé.
  4. Put your LinkedIn .zip (or old résumé) in that folder.
  5. In a local thread, mention Living Résumé and say make my résumé from my LinkedIn.

Either way, your PDF, Word file, and web page come back as downloads (they're also in that folder, under dist), and your AI helps you put the page online and update LinkedIn. Everything stays in that folder on your computer.

Questions

Straight answers.

How do I keep my résumé updated without doing it myself?

Say "add this to my résumé" when you finish something, or "catch up my résumé" and your AI suggests items from what it already knows. You approve them in a minute, and every format rebuilds from the same source.

Does it work with ChatGPT?

Yes. Install it as a plugin in the ChatGPT desktop app and use it in a local thread, with no coding. It's the same Living Résumé as in Claude: the same files, the same checks, and you approve every line.

Can it find jobs for me?

Yes, with Pro. Say what you're looking for and which companies you like. On the schedule you choose, you get the listings that fit your approved résumé best, with an Apply button on each. The search itself runs on your computer with no AI tokens. It reads public job listings only, never your email, and never logs in to a job site for you.

Will it make things up?

No. Every public line needs evidence, and new lines are proposals until you approve them. Say "make it stronger" for honest coaching: it asks, you answer, and it never invents.

Can it update LinkedIn for me?

LinkedIn doesn't allow automated profile edits, so you get a paste-ready packet trimmed to LinkedIn's limits, with what changed since last time.

Do recruiters' AI agents see it?

Your web page carries JSON Resume and schema.org Person data at a stable address, which is what search engines and agents read.
