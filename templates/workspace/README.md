# My Living Résumé

This folder is your résumé workspace. `resume/ledger.json` is the single source; every format is built from it into `dist/`.

Just talk to your AI tool (Claude, Codex, Cursor): "import my LinkedIn", "review my résumé updates", "build my résumé", "résumé status", "publish my résumé". Or run `living-resume help`.

Nothing here leaves your computer unless you publish it, and publishing is always your own commit.
